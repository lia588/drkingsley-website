About

Dr. Adebayo Kingsley Omoniyi is a lecturer, researcher, author, youth coach, pastor, and youth conference speaker with a passion for scientific discovery, education, leadership, youth development, faith, and societal transformation.

Beyond academia, he is passionate about raising purposeful young people through teaching, mentoring, coaching, conference speaking, and pastoral ministry, helping individuals develop knowledge, character, leadership capacity, and a strong sense of purpose.
